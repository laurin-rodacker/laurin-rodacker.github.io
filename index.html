01About

I hold a BSc in Mathematics from Leibniz University Hannover, specialising in partial differential equations and numerical analysis. My thesis, "A stochastic PDE system for chemotaxis," was graded 1.0.

Alongside my studies, I build quantitative tools from scratch — pricing engines in Python with no black-box dependencies, yield curve models calibrated on real Bundesbank data, and vol surface interpolation using the AAA algorithm on live SPX options. 02Selected work

Derivatives pricing

Options Pricing Engine

Analytical and semi-analytical pricing for European vanilla and barrier options. Implements Black-Scholes with full Greeks, implied volatility inversion, and Heston stochastic volatility — every formula derived from first principles.

PythonNumPySciPyHeston

Fixed income

Yield Curve Fitting

Calibration of Nelson-Siegel (1987) and Svensson (1994) yield curve models on real Bundesbank spot rate data. Pure SciPy/NumPy — no QuantLib, no sklearn.

PythonSciPyNelson-SiegelSvensson

Volatility modelling

Vol Surface Interpolation

Interpolation of implied volatility surfaces from real SPX options data using the AAA algorithm. Includes no-arbitrage verification via calendar spread and butterfly spread checks.
